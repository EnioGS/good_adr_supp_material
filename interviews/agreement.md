# Agreement between the two coders

The 14 transcripts were coded independently by two coders, in four rounds.

| Round | Transcripts | Participant turns | Jaccard | Mean κ | Mean AC1 | Codebook size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 80 | 0.622 | n/a | n/a | 45 |
| 2 | 4 | 265 | 0.781 | 0.695 | 0.970 | 64 |
| 3 | 4 | 367 | 0.944 | 0.587 | 0.979 | 72 |
| 4 | 4 | 239 | 1.000 | 0.946 | 0.997 | 76 |

- **Jaccard**: the codes proposed by both coders over the codes proposed by either, after codes that named the same idea in different words were mapped onto one name.
- **κ and AC1**: Cohen's κ and Gwet's AC1, computed per code on the participant turns of the round, before the disagreements were settled, and averaged over the codes. Codes too rare in the round for a stable estimate are left out. Both start in round 2: in the first round each coder created codes without a shared codebook.
- **Codebook size**: the number of codes once the disagreements of the round were settled.
